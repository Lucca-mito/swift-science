Protocol

DistributionWithVariance

A probability distribution that always has a variance and a standard deviation.

protocol DistributionWithVariance : DistributionWithMean

Overview

Every probability distribution with a pth moment has a (p+1) th moment. As a consequence, every DistributionWithVariance is also a DistributionWithMean. However, the converse is not true: some probability distributions have a mean but don’t have a variance.
